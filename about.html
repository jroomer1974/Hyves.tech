Domain Knowledge

Technology is only useful
when it understands the domain.

Generic software firms build platforms. We build platforms that reflect how cocoa cooperatives actually work, how citrus farmers manage orchards, and how side streams move through a value chain. That requires domain knowledge — not just code.

🍫
Cocoa
Value chain structure, farmer organisation, fermentation quality, EUDR requirements and West African supply chain dynamics.
🍋
Citrus
Smallholder orchard management, canal-based farming in Southeast Asia, peel valorisation into essential oils and animal feed, and farm data capture.
♻️
Circular Ingredients
Side-stream valorisation pathways, ingredient certification, buyer requirements and the commercial models that make circular agriculture economically viable.
🌍
Emerging Markets
Operating in markets with limited connectivity, complex cooperative structures and smallholder-first economics — across Southeast Asia, West Africa and Latin America.
We combine this domain knowledge with software, AI, hardware and circular innovation. That combination — domain first, technology second — is what differentiates Hyves from generic agrifood software firms.
What We Believe

Three principles
that shape everything we build.

01
Farmer first
Technology that doesn't improve the farmer's life is not worth building. Every decision starts with what the farmer needs, not what's technically interesting.
02
Waste is an asset
Every crop produces more than the part that gets sold. The rest is not a problem — it's a resource waiting for the right infrastructure to give it value.
03
The network is the product
One farm's data is a record. A hundred farms' data is a market. We build for the collective — because that's where the real value lives.
