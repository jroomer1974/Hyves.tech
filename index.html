Our Purpose

Create value from
agricultural side streams.

Every year, billions of kilos of agricultural side streams are discarded while farmers face increasing pressure on margins, transparency and sustainability — often without the data infrastructure to respond.

That is the problem Hyves exists to solve. Our purpose is to create new value from agricultural side streams — for farmers, cooperatives, buyers and the planet.

How We Work

One model.
Any crop, any market.

A reusable approach developed through cacao and citrus value chain projects — turning isolated smallholder farmers into connected, traceable, circular producers across emerging agricultural markets.

01
Capture Farm Data
Mobile apps, sensors, cameras and future field robotics — the right tool for the crop and context. The farmer gets visibility; we get structured data.
02
Know the Chain
Every harvest, spray decision and soil reading captured — a traceable, auditable record from field to buyer.
03
Open the Market
Traceability data enables EUDR compliance, GLOBALG.A.P. and premium pricing that isolated farmers cannot access alone.
04
Close the Loop
Peel, pulp, husk — what the farmer discards becomes a new opportunity through applied R&D: essential oils, animal feed applications and new product development.
