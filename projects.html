Cacao-Tech

Working digital traceability platform for the cacao value chain

Hyves contributed to the development of a digital traceability platform connecting farmers, cooperatives and buyers. The platform supports farmer registration, farm mapping, batch tracking, quality data capture and supply chain transparency — used in live operations across the cacao supply chain in Ecuador.

What we delivered
Farmer registration
GPS plot mapping
Batch traceability
Quality data capture (NIR)
Farmer communication module
Supply chain transparency
Built in low-code. The platform is fully configurable — every deployment is adjusted to the specific requirements of the cooperative, aggregator or supply chain operator. No fixed dashboards, no rigid product.

Citrus-Tech

Circular value creation and farm intelligence for citrus value chains in Vietnam

Exploring circular opportunities from citrus side streams — with essential oils and animal feed applications as the most promising near-term outputs — supported by farm intelligence technologies including IoT sensors, AI analytics and farm dashboards. Future field robotics in development with RMIT University Vietnam.
