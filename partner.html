How to Start

Not sure where to begin?
Here are three clear paths.

For exporters & cooperatives
Free Traceability Assessment
We review your current traceability setup and show you exactly what data you need for EUDR and buyer audits — and what you're missing.
For food companies & buyers
EUDR Readiness Scan
A structured review of your supply chain's deforestation data position — with a clear action plan to close the gaps before the deadline.
For innovation partners & investors
Discovery Workshop
A focused session to map circular value chain opportunities and identify where data infrastructure creates the most leverage for your crop or market.
